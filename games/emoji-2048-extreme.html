How to Play 2048 Emoji

Use your arrow keys (or swipe on your mobile screen) to move all emojis in a given direction across the 4x4 grid. When two emojis of the exact same type touch, they merge into a new, higher-level emoji. Every move spawns a new level-1 emoji. The game ends when the board is completely full and no more merges are possible. Try to achieve the highest score!
