Improve your emotional intelligence and learn emoji meanings with our Guess My Mood game! Look at the facial emoji presented on screen—like happy 😀, sad 😢, or angry 😡—and choose the correct textual emotion that matches it.

How to Play

A large emoji will appear in the center of the screen. Below it, you'll see several buttons with emotion words. Click the button that accurately describes the mood of the emoji. If you're correct, the game will let you know! Click "New" to try another expression.
