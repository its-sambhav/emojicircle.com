Play Emoji Sequence Memory Game

Train your brain with the Emoji Sequence Remember game! Inspired by the classic Simon memory game, this addictive challenge tests your ability to remember increasingly long patterns of flashing emojis. With both Classic mode and an intense Timed mode, it's the perfect way to improve your cognitive skills while having fun.

How to Play

When the game starts, watch carefully as the emojis flash on the 4x4 grid. Once the sequence is finished, tap the exact same emojis in the identical order. Each round you complete adds a new emoji to the pattern, making it harder to remember. If you enable "Strict Mode", a single mistake resets the game completely!
