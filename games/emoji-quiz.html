      About Emoji Quiz – Free Online Emoji Trivia Game

      Emoji Quiz on EmojiCircle is a free browser-based quiz game where you identify countries, cities, and famous places from combinations of emoji clues. The game features three difficulty levels — Easy (15 seconds per question), Medium (10 seconds), and Hard (9 seconds) — and supports 1 to 4 players. No login, no download, and no payment required.

      How to Play Emoji Quiz

      Select your difficulty level and number of players, then click Start Game. A set of emojis representing a country or place will appear. Type your answer in the input field before the timer runs out. Correct answers earn points; wrong answers deduct points on Medium and Hard modes. Use hints to reveal a clue (at the cost of 5 points). The player with the highest score at the end wins.
