Play Emoji Othello (Reversi)

Welcome to Emoji Othello, a fantastic visual spin on the classic Reversi board game! Instead of standard black and white discs, you'll be placing vibrant orange 🟠 and black ⚫ emojis on the 8x8 grid. Use your best tactics and strategic thinking to capture the board.

How to Play Reversi

The goal is to have the majority of discs turned to display your emoji color when the last playable empty square is filled. On your turn, place an emoji on the board such that there is at least one straight line (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal) of opposing emojis trapped between your new piece and another of your existing pieces. All opposing pieces in that line will flip to your color!
