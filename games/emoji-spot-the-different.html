Play Spot the Different Emoji Game

Challenge your vision and reflexes with our online Spot the Different Emoji game! In a grid full of identical emojis, one is slightly (or completely) different. As you progress, the grids get larger and the differences become subtler. Can you spot the odd one out before the timer runs out?

  • Discover nuanced differences between smiley emojis
  • Browse our collection of fun emoji puzzles

How to Play

Choose your difficulty level to start. You will see a grid filled with emojis (like 🍎 apples or 😀 smileys). Click or tap the single emoji that does not perfectly match the others. If you choose correctly, you earn points and move to the next level. If you click the wrong emoji or run out of time, you lose a life.
