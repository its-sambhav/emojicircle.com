Privacy Policy

Updated February 2026 · EmojiCircle

Policy Overview

EmojiCircle keeps things simple. We don’t require logins to play games or browse emojis, and we only collect the minimal data needed to keep the site running smoothly. This policy covers the types of information we collect, how we use it, and the rights you have to control your data.

What we collect

  • Usage analytics. Anonymous traffic insights that help us understand which games and tools are popular.
  • Device data. Basic technical details (browser, device type) used to keep the experience fast and compatible.
  • Contact messages. If you reach out to us, we store your message so we can reply.

How we use data

We use analytics to improve site performance, fix bugs, and understand feature demand. Contact messages are used solely to respond to your request. We never sell personal data and we don’t use tracked advertising pixels.

Your choices

  • Clear cookies or use private browsing to reset analytics identifiers.
  • Opt out of analytics via your browser’s “Do Not Track” setting—we respect it.
  • Email us at emojicircle.official@gmail.com to request data deletion or more detail.

Third-party services

We rely on trusted infrastructure providers for hosting and analytics. Each provider complies with industry security standards and processes data only on our instructions.

Policy Updates

If we change how we handle data, we’ll update this page with a new effective date. Keep playing and check back anytime for the latest details.
