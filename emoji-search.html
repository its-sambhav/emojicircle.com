Common Emoji Questions

What does 🥺 mean? – Pleading face, puppy eyes, expressing desire or vulnerability. What does 💀 mean? – Skull emoji, often used humorously to mean "I'm dead" (from laughter). What does 🤌 mean? – Italian hand gesture meaning "perfect" or "what do you want?" What does 🫶 mean? – Heart hands, expressing love and appreciation. What does 🙃 mean? – Upside-down face, used for sarcasm or playful irony. What does 😭 mean? – Loudly crying face, extreme sadness or emotional overwhelm (also used exaggeratedly for humor). What does 🔥 mean? – Fire emoji, meaning something is hot, trending, impressive, or exciting.
