Emoji 18.0 just moved from draft to beta — which means Unicode has finally named
every emoji in the batch. 🔍

Here's the full nine:
• Cracking Face
• Leftwards Thumb Sign
• Rightwards Thumb Sign
• Monarch Butterfly
• Pickle
• Lighthouse
• Meteor
• Eraser
• Net with Handle

Nine emoji, but nineteen characters — both thumb signs support all five skin tone
modifiers, which adds ten more.

The approval vote is set for September 2026. If it passes, the total number of
Unicode-recommended emoji hits 3,972. And then comes the wait: the gap between
Unicode approval and the emoji actually showing up on your keyboard usually runs
many months, sometimes closer to a year.
